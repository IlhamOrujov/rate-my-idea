Rate this idea honestly, as if the product were yours and your own time and money were on the line:

$ARGUMENTS

If nothing was given after the command, rate the plan or change discussed most recently in this conversation.

Answer in exactly this shape, short and concrete, using the whole 1 to 10 scale (no inflated scores):

**Rating N/10.** One sentence: would you do it, yes or no, and why.

- **Impact:** N/10, what it changes for the people who use it.
- **Effort:** N/10 (10 = trivial), what it really costs to build and keep.
- **Risk:** N/10 (10 = safe), the main way it goes wrong.
- **Timing:** N/10, why now is right or wrong.

**Strongest point:** one line.
**Biggest objection:** one line, the best argument against it.
**What would make it a 10:** one or two lines.
**Smallest version to try first:** one line, something you could ship this week to learn if it works.

Don't flatter and don't scold: rate the idea, not the person. If it's unsafe or harmful, say that instead of rating it.
